A note

I built Rumbo while looking for work myself.

Applying to everything was wearing me out, and the tools I tried did exactly what I didn’t need: they made me sound impressive with things I had never done. A recruiter reads that as fact, and in the interview they ask you about it.

So I built it the other way round. Rumbo starts from what you really did and doesn’t write a single line you can’t back up. And before you write anything, it tells you whether a job is worth it. It told me no more often than I liked. It was almost always right.

I don’t charge you because looking for work already costs enough. You only pay for the AI you use, a few cents, straight to the company that makes it. I don’t charge anything, I don’t see your data and I don’t sell it.

I hope it helps you as much as it helped me. :)

Gerardo

Works where you look

Bring the job in from wherever you find it.

On your computer, from LinkedIn

With Rumbo’s Chrome extension, a button beside each job’s title opens it in Rumbo, right alongside, ready to analyse. Move to another job and it follows.

  1. Add Rumbo to Chrome.
  2. Open a job on LinkedIn.
  3. Press “Analyse in Rumbo” beside the title.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

On your phone, from any app

Open Rumbo on your phone and install it: it shows up in the Share menu. From LinkedIn or any app, share the job, or screenshots of it, and Rumbo opens it ready.

  1. Open rumbo.korven.dev and install it.
  2. On LinkedIn, tap Share on the job.
  3. Choose Rumbo.

On iPhone, copy the job’s text and paste it in Analyse.

What it won’t do

There are things Rumbo won’t do.

  • Make things up

    If you didn’t do it, it doesn’t appear on your CV, even when that makes it less impressive. Every figure is checked against what you told it.

  • Say yes to everything

    If a job requires living in another city or a work permit you don’t have, it tells you first, before you lose the afternoon.

  • Apply for you

    It helps you decide and prepare. Sending is yours.

  • Keep your data

    Your CV and your applications live in your browser. There’s no account, and nothing of yours is stored on a server of ours.
